About EduStream

We bring South African schools into the digital age — affordably, simply, and securely.

Our Mission

To empower South African educators with a cloud platform that makes digital learning accessible, affordable, and effortless — bridging the digital divide one school at a time.

Our Vision

A future where every learner in Mzansi has equal access to quality digital education materials, regardless of their school's budget or technical resources.

Our Values

Accessibility

Every learner deserves quality education, regardless of budget or location.

Security

POPIA-compliant practices with enterprise-grade encryption.

Simplicity

Built by teachers for teachers — intuitive and stress-free.

Local First

Solutions designed for South African challenges like load-shedding.
